# RailAngyer Privacy Policy

Established: July 28, 2026
Last revised: August 15, 2026

*This English translation is provided for reference only. The Japanese version is the authoritative text.*

The developer of RailAngyer (the "App") manages the information handled by the App as follows.

## 1. Information We Collect and Store

The App stores game settings, progress records, visited stations, timestamps, and photos you take on your device.
When you use "Play Together," the following information is sent to the shared server.

- Your display name and member identifier within a room
- Room settings, turns, visited stations and timestamps
- Missions, plans, and attendance you enter
- Station photos you share

Raw GPS coordinates from your device are never sent to the shared server. Location data is used
on your device to detect arrival at stations, and when you participate in a room, only the arrived
station and the time are shared.

## 2. Purpose of Use

The collected information is used to run the walking sugoroku game, synchronize participants,
share photos and records, manage plans and attendance, and troubleshoot problems.

## 3. Usage Analytics

To identify and fix problems, the App uses TelemetryDeck to send usage data in a form that does not identify you personally.

| What is sent | Details |
|---|---|
| Types of screens and actions | Which screens were opened, which features were used |
| Course name | Which railway line is being played (to decide which course to add next) |
| Device model and OS version | To check whether a problem occurs on specific devices |
| App version | To check which release a problem occurs in |

Identifiers are hashed on your device before being sent and cannot be reversed to their original values.
**Your name, location data (coordinates and station names), photos, mission contents, display name,
and invitation codes are never sent.**

## 4. Third-Party Services

Data for the sharing features is processed by APIs, databases, and storage on Microsoft Azure.
Apple's MapKit is used to display maps and routes. TelemetryDeck is used for usage analytics.
Apple's TestFlight is used for beta distribution and feedback. Each provider's handling of
information is governed by its own privacy policy.

**The App does not serve ads and does not perform third-party tracking for advertising purposes.**

## 5. Where Data Is Stored

All data sent to the shared server (including rooms, missions, plans, attendance, and photos)
is stored on servers located in Japan (Microsoft Azure's Japan regions).
If you use the App from outside Japan, the data you send through the sharing features is
transferred across borders to Japan, where it is stored and processed.

## 6. Advertising and Tracking

The App does not display ads and does not track you for third-party advertising.
We do not sell the information we collect.

## 7. Your Rights and Deletion

Progress records and photos on your device can be erased by resetting within the App or by
deleting the App.

When you use "Leave" in a room, your photos, missions, attendance, and member information
on the shared server are deleted. Depending on network conditions, the deletion may not
complete right away. If so, the App temporarily keeps information needed to retry the
deletion (only the room ID and an authentication token — not your photos or missions) on
your device, and automatically retries for a while. This information is used only to
complete the deletion, and is removed from your device once the deletion succeeds or the
retries are given up. If it still cannot complete, the App will show you guidance in-app,
so please follow it and contact us at the address below.

If you wish to delete an entire room, or data that cannot be removed by the methods above,
contact us at the address below with the room name or invitation code. After verifying your
identity and the target data, we will respond within approximately one month of receipt.

## 8. Additional Information for Residents of the EU and the UK

If you reside in the EEA (European Economic Area) or the United Kingdom, you have rights
under the GDPR and the UK GDPR to access, rectify, and erase your data, to restrict its
processing, to object to its processing, and to data portability. To exercise these rights,
contact us at the address below. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the
supervisory authority in your region.

The legal bases for processing in the App are the performance of a contract necessary to
provide the service (providing the room sharing features) and legitimate interests
(troubleshooting and improving the service).

## 9. Security

Communication uses HTTPS, and room credentials are stored in the iOS Keychain.
We take reasonable security measures appropriate to the App's scale and the nature of the
information.

## 10. Use by Children

The App is intended for testing among family and friends. If you are of an age that requires
parental consent, please use the App with your parent's or guardian's consent.

## 11. Revisions

This policy may be revised in response to changes in features or laws. Significant changes
will be announced on this page or in the distribution notes.
