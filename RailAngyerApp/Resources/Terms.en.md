## 1. About the App

The App is a walking sugoroku (board game) in which you actually walk between railway stations.
You advance along the stations according to your dice roll, and at the station you reach,
you carry out a "mission" written by a participant.

The App is a non-commercial app developed by an individual and has no relationship whatsoever
with any railway operator.

## 2. Your Responsibility for Safety

**The App is used while walking on real roads. Ensuring your safety is your own responsibility.**

- Do not operate the screen while walking. Arrival at stations is detected automatically,
  so there is no need to look at the screen while walking
- Obey traffic laws and pay attention to surrounding traffic and pedestrians
- Do not enter restricted areas, private property, or dangerous places
- Inside railway facilities, follow the operator's instructions and posted notices
- The App is not intended to be operated inside train cars
- Stop playing as needed depending on your physical condition, weather, and temperature

The developer accepts no responsibility for accidents, injuries, damages, or troubles arising
from the use of the App.

- Content that violates laws, or encourages such violations
- Content that infringes the rights of others, or encourages behavior that causes trouble to others
- Content that encourages entering restricted areas, dangerous behavior, or nuisance behavior
- Discriminatory, insulting, or obscene content
- Personal information of third parties

**The developer does not review mission contents in advance.** The user who wrote a mission is
responsible for its content. That said, **the App has zero tolerance for inappropriate content
and abusive users.** When we receive a report, we review the content, remove it, and remove the
user who posted it from the relevant room, suspending their access.

If you find a mission that violates these terms, you can:

- Delete it within that room, if it is your own mission
- Use "Hide this person's posts" to stop seeing another user's missions on your screen
- Have the room's creator remove the abusive user from the room
- Contact us using the details in Section 12 if none of the above resolves the issue

Missions in the App are shared only among the participating companions;
there is no mechanism that publishes them to the general public.
